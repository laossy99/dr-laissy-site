01 — About

A researcher and educator working at the intersection of structures, sustainability, and intelligence.

Dr. Mohamed Yassin Ibrahim Laissy is a civil engineer, researcher, and educator whose work centers on reinforced concrete structures — how they are built, how they endure, and how they can be strengthened and rebuilt more sustainably.

His current program of research spans four connected threads: the performance and durability of concrete, sustainable and waste-derived construction materials, seismic assessment and retrofitting of RC buildings, and machine-learning models that predict and improve structural and material behaviour.

Alongside research, Dr. Laissy is deeply engaged in engineering education — curriculum development, accreditation, and quality assurance under ABET and NCAAA frameworks — building programs that prepare civil engineers for the demands of a changing profession.

  • Position Associate Professor · Head of Civil Engineering Department, College of Engineering, University of Prince Mugrin (UPM)
  • Education Ph.D. Civil Engineering, Cairo University (2010) · M.Sc. Structural Engineering, Ain Shams University (2002) · B.Sc. Civil Engineering, Cairo University (1993)
  • Focus areas RC structures · Sustainable concrete · Seismic retrofitting · AI/ML for structures
  • Also active in BIM-supported design · LCA & embodied carbon · Thermal/acoustic performance · ABET & NCAAA accreditation
  • ORCID 0000-0002-6547-8692
  • Based in Medina, Saudi Arabia

04 — Teaching

Courses that connect theory, practice, and modern tools.

Undergraduate and graduate teaching across structural analysis, reinforced concrete design, construction materials, and computational methods — with student projects that reach into current research.

  • CE 301

    Structural Analysis

    Determinate and indeterminate structures, energy methods, matrix analysis, and computer-aided modeling.

  • CE 402

    Reinforced Concrete Design

    Behaviour and design of RC members and systems following current codes, with hands-on design projects.

  • CE 415

    Construction Materials & Concrete Technology

    Materials science of concrete, durability, sustainability, and introduction to waste-derived materials.

  • CE 512

    Seismic Design & Retrofitting

    Earthquake demands, capacity design, performance-based assessment, and strengthening of existing RC buildings.

  • CE 560

    AI & Data-Driven Methods for Civil Engineering

    Applied machine learning for structural and materials problems, with datasets from research and industry.
